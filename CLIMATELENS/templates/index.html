CLIMATE SCIENCE 101

Understand Our Planet's Atmosphere

Fundamental climate principles explained for students and educators.

01

Weather vs. Climate

Weather is what happens in the atmosphere day-to-day. Climate is the regional or global pattern of weather averaged over 30 or more years.

02

The Greenhouse Effect

Gases like Carbon Dioxide (CO₂), Methane (CH₄), and Ozone trap thermal radiation within Earth's troposphere, maintaining life yet amplifying warming when excessive.

03

Air Quality Index (AQI)

AQI measures particulate matter (PM2.5, PM10) and toxic gases. Ground-level ozone and fine particles penetrate lungs and exacerbate climate-related cardiovascular stress.

04

Renewable Energy Transition

Transitioning electricity generation to solar, wind, and green hydrogen eliminates millions of gigagrams of fossil emissions, halting runaway global warming.
